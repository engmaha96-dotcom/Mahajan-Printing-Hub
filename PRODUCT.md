## Platform

web

## Users

Students, researchers, office professionals, and local businesses in Dhaka who need reliable PDF printing, binding, payment, and delivery from Nilkhet.

## Product Purpose

Mahajan IT Printing Hub lets customers upload a PDF, configure paper and finishing options, receive a transparent quote, submit delivery and payment details, and track the resulting order.

## Positioning

The service combines a practical Nilkhet print-shop workflow with an online-first order wizard, live pricing, and delivery coordination.

## Operating Context

Customers may order from mobile devices and often need quick document turnaround. The shop supports A4 and Legal documents, standard paper weights, B&W or color printing, binding, Dhaka delivery, pickup, and manual WhatsApp support for specialist jobs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- PDFs up to 50 MB can be uploaded and page-counted automatically.
- A4 and Legal are available in 70, 80, and 100 GSM.
- Printing supports B&W or color and single- or double-sided output.
- Staple binding is free; spiral is ৳70; hardcover is ৳150.
- Dhaka delivery is ৳60 flat; pickup and outside-Dhaka delivery remain available.

- Files above 50 MB and specialist services are routed to WhatsApp support at 01839802001.

## Product Principles

- Make the print order understandable before asking for commitment.
- Keep pricing explicit and itemized.
- Optimize for mobile completion and quick recovery.
- Route specialist or oversized jobs to human support.
